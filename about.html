About Us

Salary Calculator is a free educational website that provides simple, honest calculators and plain-language guides. Every tool on this site runs entirely in your browser, and no account or sign-up is needed.

We built this site because the calculations people need most are often hidden behind confusing forms or vague answers. Our calculators show the method, the inputs and the result, so you can check the answer and understand what it means.

All content on this site is written in our own words for general information. Where a guide mentions a rate, a rule or a guideline, it is labelled as an example, because rates and rules vary by country and change over time.
