Frequently asked questions

What is take-home pay?

Gross pay minus tax and other deductions such as pension contributions. It is the money that reaches your bank account.

Are the tax rates on this site real?

No. Rates shown are examples only. Tax rates, bands and allowances vary by country and change over time, so use your own country's current figures.

How do I convert hourly pay to annual pay?

Multiply the hourly wage by the hours worked each week and the weeks worked each year. At $25 an hour for 40 hours over 52 weeks, that is $52,000 a year.

What is overtime pay?

Pay for hours beyond the standard week, usually at a higher rate. The 1.5x multiplier is a common example, but rates vary by contract and country.

Why is my paycheck lower than my salary divided by 12?

Because tax and deductions are taken first. Gross monthly pay is not the money you receive; net pay is.

Should I budget from gross or net pay?

Always from net pay. A budget built on gross pay will always be too optimistic, because that money never reaches you.
